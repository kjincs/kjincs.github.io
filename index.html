I am a Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at the University of Arkansas. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC).

Research Interests

We develop programmable networking, simulation, and AI techniques to secure modern networked systems and protect critical infrastructure.

  • Cybersecurity and AI-assisted cyber defense
  • Smart-grid security and resilience
  • Programmable networks and AI-enabled network intelligence
  • Modeling and simulation of large-scale systems

We continually seek strong students to join us for Honors Thesis, Master's Thesis, and Ph.D. Dissertation research (click here for details).
