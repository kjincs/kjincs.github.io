Graduate Research Assistant Position

We have openings for strong Ph.D. students with a computer science background (or equivalent) and interests in any of the following topics:

  • Cybersecurity and AI-assisted cyber defense
  • Smart-grid security and resilience
  • Programmable networks and AI-enabled network intelligence
  • Modeling and simulation of large-scale systems

Required skills:

  • Strong self-motivation
  • Strong background in networking, systems, or AI
  • Proficient programming skills (e.g., C, C++, Java, or Python) and experience with Linux systems
  • Strong mathematical background is highly preferred

Interested students, please email the following materials to Prof. Kevin Jin. If you are already around Fayetteville, feel free to set up an in-person meeting through email.

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) or Resume
  • Transcripts (undergraduate and/or graduate)
  • Publication list (if applicable), with samples of your best publications

Undergraduate Research Opportunities

We are looking for excellent undergraduate students to participate in our research projects. Research assistantships and summer internships are available.

Preference is given to students who have already participated in our research projects or meet the following criteria:

  • Good programming skills (e.g., C, C++, Java, iOS, Android, Python)
  • Good knowledge of algorithms, computer networks, cybersecurity, or AI
  • Self-motivated, team-oriented, and deadline-oriented

Examples of How Undergraduate Students Can Get Involved:

  • Participate in an Honors College research project (first-year students are welcome)
  • Complete an Honors Thesis with Prof. Kevin Jin
  • Take Prof. Kevin Jin's courses and get excited about the topic:
    • CSCE 47503 — Computer Networks
    • CSCE 56503 — Network Security
    • CSCE 57903 — Advanced Network Security
  • Participate as a summer intern
  • Work as an hourly research assistant
